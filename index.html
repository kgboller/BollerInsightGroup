The anti-dashboard philosophy

The hard part isn't the data. It's who's reading it.

Most small and mid-size companies live in one of two states: buried in spreadsheets nobody has time to make sense of, or paying for reporting tools that produce charts and change no decisions.

Real analytics depth and real fluency with today's AI tools almost never live in the same person. Analysts who can build a model rarely know the AI stack. The AI crowd rarely has the statistical training to know when a number is lying to them.

That overlap is where I work, and I'm a business owner myself, so I optimize for the thing you care about. Not a prettier report. A better decision, made faster, with evidence behind it.
